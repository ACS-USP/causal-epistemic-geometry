# Q2 V3 Amendment 2 status

`NOT_DRAFTED — FOUR-FAMILY DESIGN UNDERPOWERED`

The principal authorization required an Amendment-2 protocol draft only if the
four-family simulations supported a sufficiently informative experiment. They
did not. Even N=400 left low maxT/full-gate power at scientifically meaningful
rho and weak discrimination of an M2 superiority margin of 0.10.

The statistical choices are fully specified for audit, but converting them
into an executable protocol would imply a level of informativeness not
supported by the planning evidence. A later protocol requires principal review
and a new source of controller/family diversity; it may not silently relax the
QAP, effect sizes, LODO, or M2-superiority criteria.

No Amendment 2 was frozen or executed.
