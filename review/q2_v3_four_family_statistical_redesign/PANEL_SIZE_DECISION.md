N=300 is the item-precision elbow. Moving from 200 to 300 reduces synthetic
rho-estimation RMSE by roughly 9–14% depending on effect/regime. Moving from
300 to 400 buys only about 5–9% more, while increasing semantic trajectories by
4,200 (33%). Neither N=300 nor N=400 repairs controller/family-level power.

The manifest is a prospective design-lock candidate, not execution authority.
Because the overall four-family design is underpowered, it must not be used
without a new principal-reviewed scientific redesign.
