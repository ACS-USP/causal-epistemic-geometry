# Q2 V3 replacement-family decision

## Decision

`PATH_B_FOUR_FAMILY`

`Q2_V3_FOUR_FAMILY_REDESIGN_REQUIRED`

No replacement family is frozen.

## Why

No candidate simultaneously satisfies all three prospective requirements:

1. enough unambiguous static source support for construction and held-out
   qualification;
2. clear mechanistic distinction from the four surviving families;
3. no relevant prior behavioral exposure capable of biasing selection.

The only candidate with adequate static positive/negative availability,
`INTERMEDIATE_DATAFLOW_COMPOSITION`, is adjacent to the behaviorally executed
Q2 V2 `STATE_TRACE` and `DECOMPOSE` policies. The cleanly novel candidates are
too sparse, while the remaining candidates are subtypes of control flow,
mutation/aliasing, loop accounting, or exact API/type reasoning.

This decision does not say that no fifth conceptual direction exists in
principle. It says that this substrate and frozen evidence history do not
identify exactly one defensible replacement without arbitrary choice or
behavioral search.

## Information used

- frozen family definitions;
- code/input text and static AST properties;
- deterministic source availability and provenance;
- the existence, but not the numerical results, of prior source-policy
  experiments;
- conceptual redundancy with the four survivors.

## Information not used

- no new model output;
- no candidate source disagreement, validity, or evaluability;
- no representations or projection gaps;
- no historical correctness, accuracy, G/C/D, rescue, or damage;
- no M0/M1/M2 values;
- no Q2 V3 primary-panel outcome (none exists).

## Consequence

The principal researcher should review a four-family redesign rather than
authorize sequential qualification of candidate replacements. Any later fifth
family would require a new explicit research decision, a new prospective lock,
and a fresh one-shot qualification authorization.
