# Q2 V3 prospective replacement-family design closeout

`Q2_V3_FOUR_FAMILY_REDESIGN_REQUIRED`

The outcome-free conceptual audit found no unique fifth family that combines
adequate static source support, mechanistic distinctness, and freedom from
relevant prior behavioral exposure. No replacement was frozen and no model
behavior was collected.

The best-populated candidate, intermediate dataflow composition, has 30
feature-positive and 58 feature-negative programs in the disjoint 88-record
pool, but is adjacent to behaviorally executed Q2 V2 state-trace and
decomposition policies. Cleanly novel candidates have only one to six positive
examples; invariant/type/order candidates are sparse, contaminated, redundant,
or all three.

A four-family redesign is algebraically viable: 16 meaningful controllers, 48
primary cross-family shell-stratified dyads, 8,400 semantic trajectories, and
an exact family-QAP universe of 2,520 labeled assignments (105 unlabeled
partitions when family names are exchangeable). It has weaker precision and
family replication than the five-family design and therefore requires a new
principal-reviewed prospective freeze rather than mechanical reuse of old
thresholds.

Historical Amendment 1 remains `Q2_V3_CONTROLLER_QUALIFICATION_FAILED` and
forensically clean. The primary relational hypothesis remains untested. Q3 was
not run.
