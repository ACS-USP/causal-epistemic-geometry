# Gate 10 adversarial premortem

Classification: `PREMORTEM_PASS`.

Gate 10 transports the byte-identical CRUXEval-derived L27 plus controller at
the Gate-8-selected D75 dose into the historical `FRESH_PSEUDOWORD_LONG`
character-count stratum. It does not reconstruct a direction, alter task
difficulty, or select items from model behavior.

The accepted generator core uses lowercase alternating consonant/vowel filler,
length 31–60, target counts 2–6, and exact integer oracles. Every preserved
historical ID, seed, generated string, and item hash is excluded; procedural
collisions are rejected. The frozen 200-item manifest is validated twice from
the strings themselves.

`character-count-semantic-v3` delegates condition-blind commitment extraction
and typed canonicalization to external-semantic-v3. It neither reads reasoning
numbers nor coerces numeric strings. Invalid, wrong-type, truncated, and
no-commitment model outputs remain primary errors.

Four new orthonormal random L27 controllers are matched to the meaningful
controller in eta, energy, duration, scope, engine, and cap. Conditions are
interleaved prospectively; all 2,800 keys and independent seeds are frozen.
Crash-safe fsync journaling prevents duplicates and retries only genuine
infrastructure failures under the same logical key and seed.

Baseline ceiling/floor is handled by a frozen opportunity gate after complete
collection. It never triggers task redesign. G/C/D remain absolute primary
estimands; G/B00 and rescue/damage opportunity ratios are descriptive
normalizations. The textual CAREFUL arm is an anchor only.

CORE_QWEN package, CUDA, model revision, parser hash, controller hashes, and
hook lifecycle must pass before collection. Any scientific ambiguity blocks;
recoverable infrastructure incidents are repaired and documented.
