# Q2 V4 — prospective intervention-subspace rationale

Status: `DESIGN ONLY — NOT FROZEN — NOT RUN`.

Q2 V3 ended because its family-based instrument was too sparse, not because its
relational hypothesis was tested and rejected. The preserved sequence is:
original freeze; provenance abort; clean provenance amendment; Amendment 1;
five-family source-gate failure; outcome-free replacement search; four-family
redesign; `Q2_V3_FOUR_FAMILY_DESIGN_UNDERPOWERED`. No V3 semantic trajectory,
M0/M1/M2 prediction matrix, or relational result exists.

V3 had only eight direction vertices and 24 cross-family dyads per shell. Its
dependent simulation found about 29% full-gate power even at nominal rho 0.40;
increasing N beyond 300 could not create controller or family units. The exact
interpretation is therefore:

\[
\text{Q2 V3 family-based relational hypothesis = UNTESTED},
\]

\[
\text{family-based instrument = INSUFFICIENTLY POWERED}.
\]

V4 changes the experimental population. Four previously source-qualified
concepts—control-flow path coverage, mutation/alias causality, loop-boundary
accounting, and hypothesis-branch elimination—each contribute prompt- and
execution-boundary directions. Those eight development-selected directions
define a local intervention subspace. New controller vertices are sampled
prospectively inside that subspace, before correctness outcomes, rather than
treating the four concept labels as inferential vertices.

The claim is deliberately local:

> For unseen intervention directions sampled from the prospectively specified
> and label-free-qualified distribution inside the development-defined
> subspace, does pre-outcome angular geometry predict semantic blind-spot
> shape after implemented-amplitude matching?

It is not a claim about unseen semantic families, all of activation space, or
transfer outside the subspace. The four concepts were selected through prior
A40 development; native-Spark qualification cannot turn them into independent
concept discovery.

Two separations govern the design:

1. implemented push size is experimentally controlled by medium/strong shells,
   while direction is tested within shell;
2. global competence shift is separated from itemwise blind-spot shape through
   a corrected two-rollout variance estimator.

No random controller outside the subspace belongs in the primary relational
population. Technical nulls may be used for engine checks, but the semantic
bank is baseline plus the two shells of the K sampled directions.

## Adversarial boundary

Arbitrary mixtures of source directions may leave the behaviorally supported
regime. V4 therefore requires, before semantic outcomes, deterministic
implemented-amplitude calibration and symmetric label-free safety/manipulation
qualification for every sampled direction at both shells. Failure stops the
instrument; it does not authorize resampling a prettier bank. Subspace
membership alone is not evidence of causal usefulness.

M3 remains `M3_DERIVATIVE_IDENTITIES_FAILED` and excluded. V4 tests only A0,
A1, and a mathematically qualified baseline-centered A2, with total finite M2
distance as a secondary decomposition.
