# Post-Q1 research roadmap

## Central question

Can internal geometry predict and engineer epistemic complementarity?

This question keeps the program coherent. Q1 establishes that fixed internal
interventions can alter semantic error profiles; Q2 asks whether control-space
geometry predicts those alterations; a later Q3 would ask whether predicted
complementarity can become deployable collective utility.

## Next — Q2 bank rebuild review

The first prospective K=16 bank failed before the shared panel: only 3/16
controllers passed the frozen causal manipulation threshold and the shuffled
nulls were not orthogonal to the meaningful span. Subject to principal approval,
qualify a rebuilt bank under a fresh lock. Only a bank that passes all source,
engineering, manipulation, and vector-geometry gates may proceed to the
controller-held-out predictive study.

Decision branches:

- If a simple metric generalizes, replicate with a larger bank and a fresh
  controller family.
- If whitened or finite-secant geometry outperforms cosine, freeze that metric
  for prospective controller selection.
- If no metric generalizes, diagnose controller diversity and measurement
  reliability before returning to exact derivatives.
- If exact local geometry is later necessary, open a separately qualified
  numerical-engine gate; do not conflate it with the first predictive test.

## Later — engineering and utility

- Test whether a frozen geometry prospectively selects a controller with the
  predicted safe complementarity.
- Only after predictive selection works, attempt constrained construction of a
  controller that maximizes predicted complementarity subject to competence and
  validity constraints.
- Replicate the predictive principle across a second model or objective domain
  when the hypothesis warrants it.
- Open Q3 only after Q2 supplies a stable complementary ensemble and uncertainty
  estimates; then evaluate routers, verifiers, or budgeted adaptive policies
  without oracle access.

## Scope-creep firewall

Do **not** pursue yet:

- a third model merely to increase model count;
- another benchmark without a frozen question and objective evaluator;
- large multi-layer or multi-dose searches selected by semantic outcomes;
- a return to exact JVP/Fisher engineering before the first Q2 prediction test
  demonstrates that such complexity is scientifically needed;
- manifold or geodesic claims without evidence that simpler metrics fail and a
  validated manifold object exists;
- Q3 routing experiments before controller-held-out geometry prediction;
- any use of confirmatory holdout rows for Q2 development.

The next scientific action is principal review of the bank-rebuild draft. This
roadmap does not authorize execution.
