# Diversification Coverage Candidate Closeout

## Status

`WITHDRAWN_BEFORE_NEW_INFERENCE`

No Qwen forward pass, generation, scientific manifest materialization, or
outcome inspection occurred for this candidate.  Spark 1 was not used for this
workstream.

## Candidate question

The proposed question was whether one canonical unsteered attempt (`A`) plus
one canonical attempt with the fixed Q1 L27-D75 controller (`B`) provided more
two-attempt coverage on a fresh procedural reasoning population than repeating
one configuration or changing only the presentation (`T`, an unsteered surface
twin).  Its proposed endpoint was

\[
G=V_{AB}-\max\{V_{AA},V_{BB},V_{AT},V_{TT}\}.
\]

The controller, the old Q2 bank, and all historical outcomes remain unchanged.

## Why the candidate was withdrawn

The same procedural law already underwent Q1 V3 Stage A baseline-only
screening and closed with zero qualifying families.  The published historical
record classifies the failure as a mix of completion/truncation limitations and
near-ceiling accuracy conditional on valid completion.  New latent IDs would
be independent observations, but they would not make this prior evidence about
the same generator distribution irrelevant.

The proposed A/T qualification could therefore approve a regime whose
apparently intermediate accuracy was driven by incomplete trajectories rather
than by a useful distribution of solvable errors.  Raising the token cap would
also create a different generation regime; it cannot be described as merely
repairing the old instrument and assumed to restore an informative coverage
question.

This is a decision to stop the coverage candidature before collection, not a
claim that the coverage estimand is invalid or that the fixed controller cannot
ever be complementary.

## Work retained

The branch retains outcome-free engineering that may be reused only after a
separate protocol has justified it:

- independent semantic auditing of canonical and surface-twin renderings;
- deterministic new namespaces and manifest hashes;
- explicit novelty audits against caller-supplied historical manifests;
- phase-disjoint schedule planning; and
- pure A/B/T coverage and qualification calculations.

Those components do not constitute a prelock, a qualified instrument, or a
scientific result.

## Next question, not yet started

A distinct candidate can make the observed failure mode its object: with the
fixed Q1 controller, does steering change the unconditional probability of a
valid and correct response within a fixed token budget, and does that effect
depend on the budget?  That study would compare steering absent/present at two
predeclared caps and retain correct, valid-wrong, incomplete, and invalid
outcomes without conditioning on completion.  It requires its own population,
budget, sample-size, endpoint, and stopping-rule prelock before any inference.
